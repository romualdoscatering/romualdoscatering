Build Your Own Catering Menu

Choose every dish yourself and see the estimate update as you go. Build Your Own Menu is for clients who want a menu that fits their theme, taste and budget exactly.

How it works

  • Enter your number of guests (minimum 30).
  • Pick your soup, appetizers, mains, pasta and dessert.
  • See your estimated price per head and total instantly.
  • Send it to us; we call you to confirm the details and send a written quotation.

Custom menus start at 30 guests with a minimum of ₱600 per head (₱18,000 total). Vegetarian, halal-friendly and other dietary needs can be arranged.
